A Bit About Me 👋

I’m a CISA-certified GRC professional and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 & 42001 Lead Auditor with nearly five years in IT governance, risk, regulatory compliance and cybersecurity audit across financial services and fintech. I work as an external consultant with CERT-In empanelled firms and currently lead ITGRC delivery for a regulated NBFC client.

Over 15+ audit and compliance engagements, I’ve delivered RBI, PCI DSS, NIST and ISO assessments across banking, cloud, infrastructure and payment-gateway environments. My work spans audit scoping and control testing, policy and framework design, vendor risk, BCP/DR, executive reporting and hands-on technical reviews with AWS, Azure, Wazuh SIEM, vulnerability management and Linux hardening.
