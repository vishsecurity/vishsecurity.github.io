--- layout: post title: "ISO 42001 & AI Risk Management" date: 2026-09-12 categories: [AI Governance, Security, Privacy] author: "Vishal Chaudhary" read_time: "7 min read" description: "Practical considerations for implementing an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS)." --- ISO/IEC 42001 provides the first international standard for establishing, implementing, and maintaining an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS). ## Core Pillars of ISO 42001 - **AI Impact Assessment:** Evaluating societal, privacy, and security implications of AI models. - **Data Governance:** Ensuring training data integrity, bias mitigation, and lineage tracking. - **Algorithmic Transparency:** Documenting model decision paths and fallback operational mechanisms. ## Integrating AI Governance into Existing GRC Rather than creating a standalone silo, map ISO 42001 controls directly into your existing ISO 27001 ISMS framework.
