Preparing for an ISO 27001:2022 audit requires more than having policies in place. The organization needs to demonstrate that its Information Security Management System (ISMS) is defined, implemented, monitored, and continuously improved.

## 1. ISMS Documentation

Before the audit, verify that the following core documents are up to date:

- ISMS Scope Statement
- Information Security Policy & Sub-policies
- Risk Assessment & Treatment Methodology
- Statement of Applicability (SoA)
- Asset Register & Data Classification Matrix

## 2. Technical Evidence Gathering

Auditors require clear traceability from controls to active technical evidence, including:

- Vulnerability Assessment & Penetration Testing (VAPT) reports
- Quarterly Identity & Access Management (IAM) reviews
- Patch management verification logs
- Cloud security configuration benchmarks (CIS benchmarks)

## 3. Management Oversight

Demonstrate executive leadership involvement through documented management review meetings, internal audit results, and tracking of security Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).
