🔐 **Bridging the Gap: SOC 2 Type II, GDPR & ISO 27701 (PIMS)**

As organizations scale globally, aligning compliance frameworks isn't just smart—it's *strategic*.

Today, we completed a detailed **mapping between SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and ISO/IEC 27701 (PIMS)**. The goal? Simplify how we demonstrate trust, transparency, and privacy across regulatory landscapes.

📌 **Why this matters:**

* **SOC 2 Type II** assures operational effectiveness of security controls.
* **GDPR** enforces data protection principles across the EU and beyond.
* **ISO/IEC 27701** (PIMS) extends ISO 27001 to build a robust privacy information management system.

✅ **Mapping highlights:**

* SOC 2's *Privacy and Security categories* align well with ISO 27701’s controls.
* GDPR’s core principles (lawfulness, data minimization, accountability) are echoed in both SOC 2 and PIMS.
* Using ISO 27701 as a bridge, organizations can operationalize GDPR obligations while maintaining audit readiness for SOC 2.

🧩 This integrated approach simplifies:

* Control harmonization
* Evidence collection
* Cross-framework reporting
